## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** resident who does not trust official civic-reporting channels, **I want to** capture a live geo-tagged photo of a local problem, tag its category, and publish it, **so that** anyone nearby can see photo-verified local issues in a proximity feed and open any report by its ID or permalink.

> This plan lays the walking-skeleton foundation (scaffold + data + test infra) that the end-to-end tracer in Plan 02 proves.

<objective>
Scaffold the greenfield Next.js 15 app, stand up the PostGIS data layer and the shared submission/feed type contract, generate and push the initial schema, and install the test harness the whole phase samples against.

Purpose: Every later task in this phase depends on the framework, the `complaints` table, the `src/types/complaint.ts` contract, and the Vitest/Playwright harness existing first. Defining the type contract here (interface-first) lets the capture slice (Plan 03) and the feed slice (Plan 04) build in parallel without editing shared files.
Output: A building Next.js app, a migrated `complaints` table, the shared type/id/db utilities, and green unit tests.
</objective>
